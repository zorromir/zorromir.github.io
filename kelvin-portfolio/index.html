01 / ABOUT

A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curiosity, grounded
in rigorous analysis.

My background in mathematics shapes the way I approach problems: understand the assumptions, build a model, test it, and investigate where it breaks.

I'm currently studying Quantitative Finance at Tilburg University, after completing a BSc in Mathematics at Utrecht University. I have independently developed a simulation-based model for an industrial company and worked with data in both research and financial coursework.
